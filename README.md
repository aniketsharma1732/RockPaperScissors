# 🪨 Rock Paper Scissors Game 🎮

A fun and interactive Rock Paper Scissors game built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. Test your luck against the computer, track your score, and enjoy the game with smooth visuals and simple logic!

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ Play Rock, Paper, or Scissors by clicking buttons or using keyboard shortcuts (`r`, `p`, `s`)
- 🤖 Random computer moves
- 🧠 Score tracking with **localStorage**
- 🎯 Visual move comparison using images
- 🔄 Reset button to clear the score

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors by clicking one of the buttons.
2. Or press:
   - `r` for Rock
   - `p` for Paper
   - `s` for Scissors
3. The result will be displayed instantly.
4. Track your **Wins**, **Losses**, and **Ties**.
5. Hit **Reset** to clear your score anytime.
